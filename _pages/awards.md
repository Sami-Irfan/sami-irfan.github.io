## Traffic Safety Scholar — 2025
**Lifesavers Conference on Roadway Safety**

Selected as one of 30 Traffic Safety Scholars nationwide and represented The University of Alabama at the 2025 Lifesavers Conference on Roadway Safety.

## Public Roads Student Writing Competition Award — 2023
**Federal Highway Administration (FHWA)**

Winner of the inaugural Public Roads Student Writing Competition. The winning article, *“Proactive Safety for Vulnerable Road Users Leveraging Digital Twin Technology,”* was featured in *Public Roads* magazine.

## ALSITE Collegiate Traffic Bowl Champion — 2023
**Alabama Section of the Institute of Transportation Engineers (ITE)**

Member of The University of Alabama team that won the 2023 Alabama Section Traffic Bowl competition against Auburn University and subsequently represented Alabama at the Southern District ITE Traffic Bowl.
