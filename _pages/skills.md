My technical work focuses on secure navigation, autonomous vehicles, and intelligent transportation systems. The following areas summarize methods and tools used in my research projects.

## Programming & Scientific Computing

- **Languages and environments:** MATLAB, Python.
- **Data processing and visualization:** pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib.
- **Technical writing:** LaTeX, Overleaf.

## GNSS, Positioning & Navigation

- **GNSS data analysis:** pseudorange and satellite measurement processing, RINEX and receiver log analysis, GNSS spoofing detection.
- **Multisensor navigation:** GNSS/INS integration, inertial mechanization, extended and unscented Kalman filtering, particle filtering.
- **Navigation integrity:** residual-based tests, chi-square testing, receiver autonomous integrity monitoring (RAIM), signal-quality evaluation.
- **Software and hardware used in research:** GNSS-SDR, software-defined radio, NovAtel GNSS/INS receiver data.

## Autonomous Vehicles & Geospatial Analysis

- **Localization and mapping:** LiDAR point-cloud analysis, map-aided localization, road network constraints.
- **Geospatial tools:** OpenStreetMap (OSM), OSMnx, QGIS.
- **Traffic simulation:** SUMO and TraCI.

## Modeling & Research Methods

- **Estimation and inference:** Bayesian filtering, sensor fusion, uncertainty modeling, statistical hypothesis testing.
- **Transportation systems:** traffic safety analytics, transportation digital twins, connected and automated vehicles, cybersecurity of intelligent transportation systems.
